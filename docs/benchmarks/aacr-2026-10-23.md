## Final results

All final groups completed 30/30 cases with zero failed cases.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| baseline | **0.4688** | 0.2913 | **0.3593** | **0.5313** | 0.4000 | 194.5 s |
| review-units | 0.3125 | **0.3884** | 0.3463 | 0.6875 | **0.4250** | 79.1 s |
| dynamic-context | 0.2189 | 0.3592 | 0.2721 | 0.7811 | 0.3243 | 81.6 s |
| snippet-location | 0.2012 | 0.3204 | 0.2472 | 0.7988 | 0.2424 | **79.0 s** |
| reflection | 0.2465 | 0.3398 | 0.2857 | 0.7535 | 0.3143 | 126.3 s |

Reflection accumulated 774,106 provider-reported tokens. `token_cost_usd` remained `null` for every group because the provider did not return a unified pricing field; no USD cost is inferred here.

## Interpretation

- In this fixed sample, baseline had the strongest F1 and lowest noise rate.
- ReviewUnit improved recall and line accuracy, but traded away precision and increased noise.
- Dynamic context, snippet location, and reflection did not exceed baseline F1 in this configuration.
- The appropriate default is therefore an engineering decision based on precision, recall, noise, and latency together—not feature complexity alone.
